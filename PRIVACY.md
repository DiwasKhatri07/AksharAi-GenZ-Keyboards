# Privacy Policy for Akshar Ai - GenZ Keyboards 🇳🇵

*Last updated: September 2026*

**Akshar Ai - GenZ Keyboards** ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by **Diwas Khatri** ([@diwaskhatri07](https://github.com/diwaskhatri07)), is engineered with an absolute commitment to user privacy, on-device intelligence, and data sovereignty.

---

### 1. Keyboard Input Handling
- **100% On-Device Typing**: Keystrokes, composing tokens, swipe paths, and word selections are processed locally in volatile memory on your Android device.
- **Zero Keystroke Recording**: The keyboard does NOT record, track, transmit, or monetize your keystrokes, personal chats, notes, or search queries.

### 2. Password & Sensitive Field Protection
- When you focus on a password, PIN, or confidential input field, Akshar Ai automatically detects the field type and shuts off:
  - Prediction engines
  - Autocorrect & dictionary learning
  - AI writing toolbars
  - Clipboard history capture

### 3. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Features
- **User-Initiated Processing Only**: Text is analyzed by AI writing modes (Groq LLaMA 3.1 / Google Gemini Flash) **strictly and exclusively** when you press an AI button (e.g., Rewrite, Expand, Translate, Gen-Z).
- **No Background Transmission**: Akshar Ai never passively feeds your typing to AI servers.
- **Offline Rule-Based Fallback**: If you do not configure an API key or when network connectivity is lost, Akshar Ai relies on a deterministic local rule engine so no data leaves your phone.

### 4. Clipboard Manager & Storage
- Up to 10 recent clipboard clips are stored locally on your device in an isolated Room SQLite database.
- You can pin favorite phrases or delete unpinned clips at any time with one tap.
- Clipboard data is never synchronized with any remote server.

### 5. Voice Typing & Text-to-Speech
- Voice recognition utilizes the native Android `SpeechRecognizer` service on your device.
- Text-to-speech leverages Android's local TTS engine without third-party audio retention.

### 6. Analytics & Telemetry
- Akshar Ai contains **NO third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or telemetry beacons**.
